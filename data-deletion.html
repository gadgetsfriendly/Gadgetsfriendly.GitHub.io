GadgetsFriendly respects your privacy and provides a way for customers to request deletion of their accounts and eligible personal information.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@mail.gadgetsfriendly.com.ng.
  2. Use the subject line Personal Data Deletion Request.
  3. Tell us the email address associated with your account and whether you want your account closed, your personal information deleted, or both.
  4. We may contact you to verify your identity before processing the request. Please do not send your password, payment-card details or other sensitive credentials by email.
Request by email:
Email GadgetsFriendly Support

What may be deleted?

Depending on the request and applicable law, we may delete or anonymise account profile details, contact information, optional birthday information and other personal information that is no longer required.

Information we may need to retain

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where necessary to meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, maintain transaction records, or administer warranties and services already provided. Where appropriate, retained information will be restricted to the relevant purpose.

Processing your request

We will review your request, verify it where necessary and respond within the period required by applicable law. If we cannot delete particular information, we will explain the reason where legally appropriate.

Orders and repairs

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an order, reverse a payment, cancel an existing repair arrangement or remove a legal obligation. Please mention any outstanding order or repair in your request so we can explain the available options.
