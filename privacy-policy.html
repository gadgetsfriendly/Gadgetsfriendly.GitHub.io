Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

GadgetsFriendly respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our website, create an account, purchase products or request repair services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Account information needed to create and secure your customer account.
  • Delivery address and information needed to process an order.
  • Product purchases, order references, payment status and receipt details.
  • Repair requests, device information and details you provide about a fault.
  • Product serial numbers or IMEI identifiers where required for inventory, warranty, fulfilment or customer service.
  • Your date of birth if you choose to provide it, and your birthday-email preference if you opt in.
  • Technical information such as browser type and basic usage information where collected by our website or service providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Process purchases, verify payments and provide receipts.
  • Arrange deliveries and communicate order updates.
  • Receive repair requests and provide repair-related updates.
  • Maintain appropriate product, warranty and service records.
  • Respond to enquiries, prevent fraud and protect our services.
  • Send birthday messages only where you have opted in.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed by Paystack or another payment provider made available at checkout. Payment providers handle payment information under their own privacy and security practices. GadgetsFriendly uses relevant transaction information, such as payment status and order reference, to manage orders and receipts.

We do not intend to collect or store your full payment-card details in our website database.

4. Account and Device Security

We use service providers and technical safeguards to help protect customer accounts and business records. Access to private product identifiers, including serial numbers and IMEI details, should be restricted to authorised operational purposes.

No website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a strong password and never share your account credentials.

5. Service Providers and Sharing

We may share relevant information with trusted providers when necessary to operate our services, including:

  • Supabase for database, account authentication and related website services.
  • Paystack for payment processing.
  • Resend or another configured email provider for receipts and service emails.
  • Delivery providers and professional advisers where required to fulfil an order or meet a legal obligation.

We aim to share only information needed for the relevant purpose. We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect our rights and customers.

6. Email and Marketing Preferences

We may send necessary transactional messages, including account verification, password-reset messages, purchase receipts and repair updates.

Optional birthday messages or promotional communications should only be sent in accordance with your preferences and applicable law. You may withdraw optional email consent by contacting us. Necessary service messages may still be sent when required to provide a service you requested.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to support functions such as shopping carts, account sessions, preferences and security. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling some technologies may affect website functionality.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order fulfilment, repairs, warranties, account management, security and applicable legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and its purpose. Information that is no longer needed should be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion where appropriate, or withdrawal of consent for optional processing. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security or contractual reasons.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for customers able to use them under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information unlawfully. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us to request appropriate action.

11. External Websites

Our website may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review those policies before providing personal information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date where appropriate.
